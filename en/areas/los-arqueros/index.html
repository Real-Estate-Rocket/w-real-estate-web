What is Los Arqueros?

Los Arqueros is a golf development in the municipality of Benahavís, on the road climbing from San Pedro de Alcántara towards Ronda. It is organised around Los Arqueros Golf, an 18-hole, par 71 course designed by Severiano Ballesteros and opened in 1991. It sits on a slope with views over the valley and the sea, and is one of the Benahavís golf developments with the quickest access to the coast.

What kind of property is there in Los Arqueros?

Apartments and townhouses in complexes with pools around the course, and villas on the higher plots. The area offers a good balance between price per square metre and setting, and has rental demand from golfers out of season.
